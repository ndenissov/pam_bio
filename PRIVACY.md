**1. General Provisions & Developer Information**  
This Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as the "Policy") governs the data processing practices for the application **PAM Bio** (package `ovh.dep.pam`, hereinafter referred to as the "Application"), authored and maintained by developer **Nikita Denissov** (hereinafter referred to as the "Developer"). By using the Application, you agree to the terms of this Policy.

**2. Data Collection and Processing**  
The Application **does not collect, transmit, distribute, or otherwise process any personal data**, user identifiers, device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics. All computations and data processing required for the Application's functionality are performed exclusively locally on the user's device and over the user's local network (LAN/Wi-Fi) to communicate with the user's paired computer. No user data is ever sent to external servers or cloud services.

**3. Permissions and Usage**  
The Application requests only the minimal permissions required to provide its core functionality:
- **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`):** Used solely to scan pairing QR codes displayed on your computer when linking a new device. The image stream is processed entirely locally in real-time. No photos or videos are captured, saved, or uploaded. You may safely revoke camera access after pairing is complete.
- **Biometric Hardware (`android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC`):** Used via official Android system APIs (`BiometricPrompt`) to prompt for local fingerprint or facial authentication. The Application never accesses, reads, or stores your biometric raw templates.
- **Notifications (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used strictly to maintain the persistent background service and deliver incoming PC authentication requests. No marketing or tracking notifications are ever sent.
- **Full-Screen Alerts (`android.permission.USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT`):** Used to display authentication prompt overlays on the lock screen for convenient approval.
- **Local Network Access:** Used exclusively for direct, peer-to-peer end-to-end encrypted communication (X25519 ECDH, Ed25519, AES-256-GCM) with the `pambiod` daemon on your local Wi-Fi / LAN. No third-party network connections are made.

**4. Data Storage and Security**  
All cryptographic keys and paired device information generated by the Application are stored exclusively within the protected private local storage of the user's device. The Developer (Nikita Denissov) has no access to user data, does not back up data to remote servers, and does not share any data with third parties.

**5. User Identification and Tracking**  
The Developer does not physically identify and does not possess the technical capability to identify users of the Application. The Application contains no third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics SDKs.

**6. Changes to the Privacy Policy**  
The Developer reserves the right to amend this Policy as necessary to reflect updates in the Application or applicable laws. Continued use of updated versions of the Application constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
