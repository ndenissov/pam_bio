Tired of typing your long password every time you invoke "sudo" in Linux or unlock your screen in Windows? PAM Bio solves this by allowing you to use your smartphone's biometrics for instant, secure PC authentication.

PAM Bio turns your Android smartphone into a full-featured wireless hardware security key. Unlock your desktop (LogonUI on Windows, SDDM/GDM on Linux), authenticate sudo requests, and log in using your phone's fingerprint, face recognition, or PIN. It's the ultimate free alternative to hardware security tokens!
